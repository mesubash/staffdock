# Privacy

StaffDock is software you install on your own Frappe site. It does not collect, send or share any data
with the StaffDock project or anyone else.

All information (employees, attendance, leave, expenses, requests, assets and files) stays in your
site's database and file storage, under your control and your own privacy policy. Emails are sent only
if you turn on email notifications, through the email account you configure.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mesubash/staffdock/issues.
